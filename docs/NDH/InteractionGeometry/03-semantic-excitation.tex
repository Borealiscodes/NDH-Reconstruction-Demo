\section{Semantic Excitation}
\label{sec:semantic-excitation}

Semantic excitation models the
dynamic update of the state vector
$\mathbf{z}(t)$ and the metric tensor
$g_{ij}$ under an incoming stimulus
perturbation vector field $V_{\text{perturb}}$.
Rather than allowing unconstrained
linear shifts, the deformation of the
latent metric over time is governed by the
forced-Ricci-flow-inspired evolution law:
\[
\frac{\partial g_{ij}}{\partial t}
= -2 R_{ij}
+ \alpha \nabla_i V_{\text{perturb},j}
+ \beta F_{ij}
\]
where $R_{ij}$ is the Ricci curvature
tensor, $F_{ij}$ is an external forcing
tensor, and $\alpha, \beta$ denote the
systemic susceptibility coefficients.

To ensure that structural deformation
remains strictly controlled and does not
induce representational failure, the magnitude
of the perturbation field must be strictly
bounded by the manifold's local stability
scalar $D(x,t)$:
\[
\|V_{\text{perturb}}\| < D(x,t)
\]
The dynamic response of the metric
with respect to the perturbation field
is explicitly quantified by the
Susceptibility Tensor $S_{ij}$:
\[
S_{ij} = \frac{\partial g_{ij}}
{\partial V_{\text{perturb}}}
\]
This operational profile ensures that
dynamic energy transitions preserve
structural continuity and remain bounded
away from the collapse surface.
