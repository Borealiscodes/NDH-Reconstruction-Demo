\section{Semantic Excitation}
\label{sec:semantic-excitation}

Semantic excitation models the
dynamic update of the state vector
$\mathbf{z}(t)$ and the metric tensor
$g_{ij}$ under an incoming stimulus
perturbation vector field $V \in \Gamma(TM)$.
Because the metric tensor is symmetric,
the deformation of the latent space
over time must be governed by a
symmetrized, forced-Ricci-flow law:
\[
\frac{\partial g_{ij}}{\partial t}
= -2 R_{ij}
+ \alpha \left( \nabla_i V_j 
+ \nabla_j V_i \right)
+ \beta F_{ij}
\]
where $R_{ij}$ is the Ricci curvature
tensor, $F_{ij}$ is the external forcing
tensor, and $\nabla$ denotes covariant 
compatibility along the active domain.

To maintain structural stability, the
dynamic response of the metric to
this field is explicitly tracked via the
Susceptibility Tensor variation $S_{ij}(V)$:
\[
S_{ij}(V) = \lim_{\epsilon \to 0} 
\frac{g_{ij}(V + \epsilon \delta V) 
- g_{ij}(V)}{\epsilon}
\]
This formulation ensures that
high-energy state transitions preserve
algebraic composability, matching the
strict Jacobi identities required by the
underlying operator fields.
