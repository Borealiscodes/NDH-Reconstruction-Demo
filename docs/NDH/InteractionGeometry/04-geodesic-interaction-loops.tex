\section{Geodesic Interaction Loops}
\label{sec:geodesic-loops}

Long-range context preservation 
during sequential interaction 
requires tracking how controlled 
perturbations deform path-dependent 
structures over time. We formalize 
this representational drift across 
a closed loop trajectory $\gamma$ 
using the Holonomy Drift Tensor $H(\gamma)$ 
derived in our master framework:
\[
H(\gamma) = \oint_{\gamma} 
\nabla V_{\text{perturb}}
\]
where $\nabla V_{\text{perturb}}$ represents 
the covariant derivative of the 
perturbation vector field inducing 
deformation on the latent metric.

The magnitude of the accumulated 
connection asymmetry along $\gamma$ 
is strictly bounded by the manifold's 
local stability scalar $D(x,t)$. 
If the tension vector induced by 
recurrent sequence steps violates 
this threshold, the tracking trajectory 
breaches the collapse surface boundary:
\[
\|V_{\text{perturb}}\| \ge D(x,t)
\]
The system regulates this boundary 
by continuously feeding error tensors 
back into the 9D radial Laplacian, 
forcing the localized deformation 
to remain asymptotically stable 
and drift-neutral over extended 
operational horizons.
