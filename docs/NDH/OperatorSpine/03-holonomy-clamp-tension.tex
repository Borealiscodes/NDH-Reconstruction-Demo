\label{sec:holonomy-tension}

interaction loops.

\[
\mathcal{\kappa}_H(\gamma)

\mathcal{H}(\mathbf{z}) \,
d\gamma \right\|
\]
where $\mathcal{H}(\mathbf{z})$
denotes the holonomic connection form
characterizing the manifold topology.

\[
\mathcal{T}_H

\left( \Delta_9
\mathcal{\kappa}_H(\gamma)
\right) \cdot
\nabla_{\mathrm{apex}}
\mathcal{E}(x) \, d\mathcal{M}
\]

anomalies, enforcing dynamic
