\section{Apex Gradients}
\label{sec:apex-gradients}

Apex gradients represent the
normalized steepest ascent
direction of the operator-spine
energy functional $\mathcal{E}$
in the 80-dimensional
embedding space, defined as:
\[
\nabla_{\mathrm{apex}} \mathcal{E}(x)
= \frac{\nabla \mathcal{E}(x)}
{\|\nabla \mathcal{E}(x)\|}.
\]
They govern NDH's geometric
response to excitation,
regulating manifold stability,
save-state reversibility,
and holonomy tension.

By interacting with the 9D
radial Laplacian:
\[
\Delta_9 f(r)
= f''(r) + \frac{8}{r} f'(r)
\]
apex gradients balance
directional deformation
with radial curvature diffusion
to maintain drift neutrality
and structural coherence.
