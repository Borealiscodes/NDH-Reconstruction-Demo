\section{Operator Algebra}
\label{sec:operator-algebra}

Geometric operations use a bounded, 
deterministic operator algebra $\mathcal{A}$ 
acting over the 80-dimensional continuous 
manifold $\mathcal{M}$. Mappings are grouped 
into directional operators $D_v$, diffusion 
operators $L_r$, and projective 
operators $P_{\Sigma\Lambda}$.

Simultaneous transformations are 
governed by the Lie commutator, evaluating 
the operational friction between 
directional steering and radial diffusion:
\[
[D_{\mathrm{apex}}, L_{\Delta}]
= D_{\mathrm{apex}} \circ L_{\Delta} 
- L_{\Delta} \circ D_{\mathrm{apex}}
\]
To maintain stability under continuous 
perturbation, the commutator is 
bounded directly by the loop integral of 
the holonomic connection form:
\[
[D_{\mathrm{apex}}, L_{\Delta}]\,\mathbf{z} 
= \oint_{\gamma} 
\mathcal{H}(\mathbf{z}) \, d\gamma
\]
Sequential compositions 
$\mathcal{O} = A_n \circ \dots \circ A_1$ 
remain inside strict global safety 
envelopes, preventing un-governed 
contextual collapse or gradient explosions.
